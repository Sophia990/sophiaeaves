Manicure Gala

Manicure Gala is a web-based nail painting game, aimed at young children, which I am currently developing in association with Dream Reality Interactive, and plan to release on Poki. The video below shows a prototype version of the game.

Development

In Manicure Gala, you can decorate a set of nails at a nail salon, using nail polishes and a selection of acrylic patterns and gems. The colours of the polishes can be changed and you are able to use a fill tool. You are also able to focus on individual nails and clear your designs.

Additionally, there is a multiplayer mode, which is currently mocked in the prototype, in which you compete against other players to make a nail design in two minutes, and everyone votes for their favourite design at the end.

I am developing this game in Unity 6, and have needed to carefully consider various aspects of the design and implementation, particularly with regards to the user interface. I decided that it would be most intuitive to select a polish bottle and drag on the nail to paint, to drag and drop for the gems, and to select and click to place an acrylic pattern.

Moreover, the game subtly shows the player which polish or acrylic is selected by making the icon slightly bigger. Holding a different polish bottle to change its colour will also automatically select that polish bottle, as I felt this was what the player would most likely expect to happen.

I also considered how the UI would need to adapt to different screen sizes, including a mobile screen. The game therefore displays only one hand at a time if the screen height is greater than the screen width. In addition to this, I enlarged all UI buttons so that they could be pressed easily with a finger on a small touchscreen.
